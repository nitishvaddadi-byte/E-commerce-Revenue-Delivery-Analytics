# E-commerce Revenue & Delivery Analytics

## About the Project
This project analyzes the Olist Brazilian e-commerce dataset to understand sales performance, revenue trends, product categories, state-wise performance, delivery delays, and payment reconciliation.

## Problem Statement
The goal of this project is to understand how an e-commerce business performs in terms of revenue, sales, and delivery operations, and to identify areas that need improvement.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- SQL
- Power BI
- DAX
- Excel

## Dashboard Features
- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Late Delivery Rate
- Average Delivery Days
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Revenue by Product Category
- Revenue by Region
- Top 5 States by Revenue
- Payment Reconciliation
- Late Delivery by State
- Year, Month, and State filters

## Key Findings
- Monthly revenue trends help identify changes in business performance.
- Revenue varies across product categories and states.
- Delivery performance differs across states.
- Payment reconciliation helps compare order item values and payment records.
- Freight costs and delivery delays need further investigation.

## Business Recommendations
- Identify states with higher delivery delays and improve logistics.
- Analyze product categories to understand revenue contribution.
- Monitor monthly revenue trends for better business planning.
- Investigate high freight costs and payment mismatches.
- Include product costs and operating expenses to calculate actual profitability.

## Project Limitation
The available dataset does not contain complete product costs and operating expenses. Therefore, this project focuses mainly on revenue and delivery analytics rather than actual net profit.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates how Python, SQL, and Power BI can be used to analyze e-commerce data, build an interactive dashboard, and generate useful business insights.
